    Blowdown is the gap between the pressure at which a safety valve opens (#set_pressure) and the lower pressure at which it finally closes (#reseat_pressure), usually given as a percentage of the opening pressure .
    Why is the reseat pressure lower than set pressure ?
    The valve must wait for the pressure to drop a bit below the opening point so it won’t rapidly snap open and shut (called “chattering”).
    This extra drop lets the spring and seal overcome friction before the valve fully closes .

  • 04-19 07:13 刚发现 Vercel 另一个好东西 - waitUntil()。
    简单来说，就是可以把异步执行的操作放在 waitUntil() 执行，这样既不影响响应的发送，又能在后台完成必须的操作。

    - 立即响应支付网关 webhook，数据更新放waitUntil执行，因为立即响应了，就会减少重复触发事件的几率
    - AI生成图片、视频耗时太长，其实也可以设计成异步的，在waitUntil里面调用第三方API
